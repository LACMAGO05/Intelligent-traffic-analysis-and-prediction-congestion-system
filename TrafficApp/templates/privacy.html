Privacy Policy

How Traffik Intelligence collects, uses and protects your data.

What we collect

  • Account data: username, email, and a securely hashed password.
  • Route data: the origins and destinations you predict or watch for alerts.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address for new-device sign-in alerts.
  • Device info: browser/user-agent and push-notification subscriptions you enable.
  • Usage data: predictions made and basic analytics events.

How we use it

To generate traffic predictions, send the gridlock and security alerts you opt into, secure your account against unauthorised access, and improve the service through aggregate analytics. We do not sell your data.

Third parties

We share the minimum necessary data with Google Maps (routing), OpenWeatherMap (weather context), and SendGrid (email delivery). These providers process data under their own policies.

Your choices

You can remove watched routes and trusted devices, disable notifications, and request account deletion at any time by contacting us. Logging out signs you out of all devices.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us at traffik147@gmail.com.

This is a student research project (University of Buea). Data is used for academic and service-improvement purposes only.
